## Corrections and source handling

- Des Walker’s club list in the source incorrectly names Juventus. It is corrected to Sampdoria; his [career record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Des_Walker) supports that correction.
- The history claims Baroness Burdett-Coutts gave Victoria Park to the public. It now says she donated the drinking fountain, consistent with [Tower Hamlets’ history of Victoria Park](https://www.towerhamlets.gov.uk/lgnl/leisure_and_culture/parks_and_open_spaces/victoria_park/history.aspx).
- Jaidon Anthony’s Brentford entry was checked and retained; [Brentford’s announcement](https://www.brentfordfc.com/en/news/article/first-team-brentford-sign-jaidon-anthony-burnley-transfer) supports it. Player club lists are presented as selected career associations, not live current-club data.
- Spelling, punctuation, obvious broken encoding, “20-26-27” and “Kwik Crcket” were cleaned up without inventing missing facts.
- The alumni Word document’s “Join us” and donation links point to `heatholdboys.org.uk`, another organisation. They were replaced by the on-site alumni section and HSAA contact route.
- `District Competitions.pptx` is mislabelled: it duplicates the other-sports programme. That content is included under Other Sporting Competitions. District competition content instead uses the district team descriptions, Inner London blurb, results instructions and Lester Finch archive.
- PowerPoint layout instructions, the ticker example website, placeholder link text and “DANIELE” instructions are implemented where applicable, rather than displayed as public copy.
- The legacy HTML and old sitemap are reference material. Their obsolete navigation, counter scripts and historic supporter link were not reintroduced into the updated site.
- All 17 public subject/logo images were included; the eighteenth image is the old sitemap, which was retained as reference. Embedded supporter/foundation logos were extracted for the relevant pages.
- No social credentials, raw presentations, extraction data or private audit material is included in `dist`. The only Office download is the reviewed, blank alumni membership form. Six October 2026 PDFs are also available.

## Forms and news

School results support the boys’ and girls’ Nine a side Leagues. District results support the Kay Trophy, John Larter Girls District Trophy and Lester Finch Trophy. The organisation label and prepared email distinguish school from district.

Both result forms and the alumni form open the visitor’s email app for review and sending. They do not submit to a server, store entries, take payment or automatically update public results. A functioning email app is required. The supplied source asks for a direct email route; a server-backed service would need a separate receiving/storage setup.

The ticker is maintained in `content/pages/index.html`. The source asks for weekly/fortnightly updates but supplies no automated feed or publishing process. The site retains manually editable, dated news and retains reduced-motion support; the pause/resume button was removed at the user's request.
